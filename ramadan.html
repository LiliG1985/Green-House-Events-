Cultural Dance

Traditional dance from across the Arab world, perfect for welcome moments, feature performances and Eid celebrations.

Sufi Dance

Whirling Sufi performers in flowing white robes, a calm, spiritual and mesmerising moment that suits the reflective spirit of the holy month.

Tanoura Dance

Dancers spinning in vibrant, layered skirts that bloom into colour, a traditional Egyptian spectacle set to live music.

An Emirati Ayala troupe performing the traditional line dance with canes and drums, a proud celebration of UAE heritage.

High energy Levantine folk dance with lively footwork and joyful formations that get guests clapping along.

Gulf dancers in colourful traditional dress performing the graceful, hair tossing Khaleeji style in short sets through the evening.
