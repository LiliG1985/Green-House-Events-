Who We Are

Dubai's most complete
event house

Greenhouse Events is Dubai's premier full-service event production company. We bring together creative vision, technical mastery and meticulous planning under one roof — so every event feels effortless and every moment is unforgettable.

From the very first conversation to the moment your guests leave, we handle every detail. Our in-house team of designers, AV engineers, production managers and entertainment curators work as one seamless unit.
